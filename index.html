Access & Security

  • Sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication; roles are enforced by Firestore security rules on the server.
  • Each student QR holds a long random secret code, not the LRN, so QR codes cannot be guessed or forged.
  • Address, contact, guardian and photo are visible to the Administrator only.

Registration Information

Each student gets a unique, random QR code. It is stored in the database and checked on every scan; the LRN is not inside the QR.
Lost or stolen ID? Open the student below with ID / QR and press Regenerate QR — the old QR stops working immediately.

Attendance Rule

A valid scan in the morning Time In window marks the student Present; a scan in the afternoon Time Out window records departure. The times are set in Edit System. The next day starts fresh automatically.
